Privacy & cookies

Last updated 2026-08-21

Cookies

This site sets none. There is no login, no analytics and no advertising, so there's nothing for a cookie to do here.

What's stored on your device

Your theme (ink or paper), your chosen layout (grid or index), and any items you've starred are saved in your browser's localStorage. This never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Clearing your browser data clears it.

What we don't collect

No accounts, no forms, no tracking, no server-side logs tied to you. We don't know who visits this page.

One external request

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser makes a request to Google when you open this site. That request is subject to Google's own privacy policy, not ours.

This is a small link directory, not an application — there's no account to create and nothing to agree to, so there are no separate terms of service.
